# Instruments on lines: the map, the line popup and the studio (contract 0.3.0; the studio's 0.4.0)

How Map Studio draws a tap, meter or line valve on its line, splits the line into segments, grades each segment and opens a line's popup (epic 3 entry 2, R13; architecture AD-7, AD-11, AD-16, AD-19; DESIGN.md line-popup, map-line, instrument-marker). Storing the attachment is [contract.md](contract.md), "Instruments on lines"; the `segments` slot of `values.batch` is there too, "The segments slot"; the kind setup's instruments source is [kind-setup.md](kind-setup.md). Labels: **[live 8.3.10]** seen on the dev stack (`e2e/tests/linepopup.spec.mjs`; [platform-checks.md](platform-checks.md), "Epic 3 entry 2"); **[build]** the unit tests; **[ER-27]** goes to the engineering review of entry 12.

## Nodes and segments

A line's **nodes**, in order from its first vertex, are the item its start snaps to (`ends.start.itemId`, or the floating start), each attached instrument that **measures**, in order of its `attachment.distanceM`, and the item its end snaps to (`ends.end.itemId`, or the floating end). An instrument measures when its kind setup has an icon KPI that is on (`KindDisplay.iconKpi`, the same rule in `web/typescript/live.ts`): a tap with a pressure KPI measures; a line valve whose setup has comms and a position KPI with `icon: false` does not. A **segment** runs between two consecutive nodes. On the dev stack's L-SWD (CTB1 to SWD1, PT-1 at 1,143 m, LV-1 at 2,000 m, PT-2 at 2,841 m of 2,922 m) the nodes are CTB1, PT-1, PT-2, SWD1 and the segments CTB1 to PT-1, PT-1 to PT-2 and PT-2 to SWD1; LV-1 sits inside PT-1 to PT-2 **[live 8.3.10]**.

Both sides apply the same rule, and each does its part (plan decision 3):

- **The Gateway grades.** Once per line per batch build, in the shared live-value cache, a line with instruments whose style rule is the instruments source gets a `segments` slot: per segment the worse of the two end instruments' presets, `stale` when either end is stale (the first named in `reason`), and the worse active end alarm; the line's own `style` slot is the worst over them. No geometry is measured on the Gateway for this, and no session does the work **[live 8.3.10]**, **[build]** (`Segments`, `InstrumentsLiveTest`).
- **The browser cuts the geometry.** `features.ts` cuts the line's coordinates at the measuring instruments' distances into one feature per segment (derived from the attachments, never stored), carrying `from` and `to` and the look its `segments` entry gives, matched by those ids; a segment the batch does not name, and every segment of a line without a `segments` slot, draws the line's own look. A click on a line therefore lands on a segment feature **[live 8.3.10]**, **[build]** (`segments.test.ts`).

## What the map draws

| Part | Look |
| --- | --- |
| An instrument | Its kind's marker at the point its attachment stores: a 24 px neutral disc with the kind glyph (DESIGN.md instrument-marker, the same image box and anchor as a point marker, the image id with an eighth element), its name below at 11 px, the state chip, the icon KPI, and its own alarm badge and digit: **an instrument's alarm draws at its place on the line**; the line's own alarm keeps the midpoint badge. The two instruments at an open segment's ends take the selection ring. Instruments join the 20 m shared-marker sweep like any point **[live 8.3.10]** |
| A segment | The line's weight by pipe size (3, 4, 6 px by `diameter`). With the instruments source: the preset's hue and word for the worse end instrument, dashed with the stale look when either end is stale, the segment's end alarm's hue over both; the line's own alarm over everything. The line's chip at the midpoint carries the worst word ("L-SWD Stale", "L-SWD Attention") **[live 8.3.10]** |
| The open segment | Firmer selection casing than the rest of the selected line; the map's `data-segment` and `data-highlighted` say which **[live 8.3.10]** |

Comms lost and stale: a stale end instrument dashes its two segments and nothing else; its own marker carries the comms-lost or stale chip. The hue of a segment's preset and alarm are the tokens of [styles.md](styles.md); instrument alarms and the aggregate look are **[ER-27]** items.

## The line popup

In View mode a click on a line whose kind setup shows anything, or that carries an instrument, opens the line's default popup at the clicked segment (in Edit mode a click selects the line with Details open and its handles, no popup and no segment: [contract.md](contract.md), "A click in Edit mode"), 350 px wide (`line-popup-width`), built to the padding rules of the popup review (16 px sides, the header `12px 12px 8px 16px`, 32 px icon buttons and 44 px below 768 px, `max-height: min(480px, map - 64px)`, the tip in the card surface, the floating shadow), placed inside the map less the top bar and the zoom card as the point card is (owner review notes item 18). The header and parts 2, 3 and 5 stay pinned at the top; only the instrument list (4) scrolls, about four rows, the highlighted rows scrolled into view (item 19). In order:

1. **Header**: the pipeline glyph in a disc, the name, the subline (kind, pipe size, length: "Gathering line - 6 in, 2.9 km"), the page link slot, a close button (MapLibre's own close button is hidden for this popup).
2. **The segment box** (when a segment is open on a line with a measuring instrument; a line with none has no segment to show, item 17; `selection-tint` with a `selection` edge): "Segment PT-1 to PT-2", its span "1.1 to 2.8 km", and one line: "398 to 371 psi - drop 27 psi over 1.7 km" when both ends carry a number in one unit (rise, drop or no change), the stale glyph and "Stale - drawn dashed until PT-1 updates" when an end is stale, else the two values as words.
3. **Comms** over the instruments, short so the row stays on one line at the card's width: "OK - 3, oldest 9 s", or with an instrument's comms lost "Comms lost - 1 of 3" (before stale), or with a stale one "Stale - 1 of 3"; the full sentence ("OK - 3 instruments, oldest 9 s ago", "Comms lost on 1 of 3 instruments: PT-1, last 18 min ago") in the row's tooltip and under the row on a tap, as the point card's rows do (owner, 0.3.0 re-test); with no instrument on the line the line's own comms row where its setup turns comms on.
4. **Instruments, CTB1 to SWD1** (drawn after the summary, the one scrolling part): one row per node in order: a snapped end as "CTB1 outlet" or "SWD1 inlet" with a filled square (a floating end "Line start" or "Line end"), an instrument with its kind glyph; the distance in tiny type ("0 km", "1.1 km", "2 km"); the value right-aligned: the icon KPI ("398 psi"), "No value" (no icon KPI, or none yet), or the stale glyph before the last value ("371 psi") with the reason in the row's tooltip ("Stale - last value, comms lost"); an active alarm's glyph and digit before it. The open segment's two instruments are highlighted (`selection-tint`, a 3 px `selection` edge), the same two that carry the ring on the map.
5. **The line's own KPIs** (up to three) and the **alarm summary**: the instruments' active alarms (from `values.batch`) and the line's own, naming the instrument in the worst alarm ("1 alarm on the line - PT-2, Priority 1, unacknowledged", the others after it: "Also: PT-1 priority 2, acknowledged"); where the line's setup turns alarms on and nothing is active, "No active alarms" with no check mark, the hourglass before it while its comms are lost or its alarms stale.

While it is open the Gateway pushes the line and every listed instrument within 250 ms of a change (PT-2's row followed its tag in under a second on the dev stack), the popup clock refreshes the ages, and a new click on another segment of the same line sends a new `popup.open` with that segment. Closing it (the header button, the ground) sends `popup.close` and clears the line's selection and the highlight **[live 8.3.10]**. At 390 px the card is the same, `calc(100vw - 32px)` wide with 44 px header buttons, inside the viewport; the bottom sheet is entry 8's.

A line kind's custom popup view (the kind setup's `popupView`, entry 8; a missing or failing view falls back to the line popup) gets the line's map item object (entry 8's `{id, name, kind, assetId, properties, basePath, alarmsPath, values}`) with `instruments: [{id, name, kind, distanceM, liveValues}]` in order along the line and the open `segment: {fromId, toId}` (AD-7).

## Reshaping and splitting a line (epic 4 entry 5)

A line's shape changes in Edit mode ([contract.md](contract.md), "Changing geometry"): a point dragged, or moved by Move then a click, a midpoint dragged in, a point deleted, or a point item moved that the line's `ends` name (its first or last point follows). Each is one `edits.apply` batch with the line's new geometry and, for each instrument on it, an update to its nearest place on the new shape: `attachment.distanceM` measured again from the line's first point and the point at that distance (`web/typescript/geometry.ts` `lineGeometryBatch`); the Gateway checks every instrument within 1 m of its place and refuses a line batch that leaves one out. An instrument does not ride on a point: it keeps its nearest place, so a point dragged away near an instrument leaves the instrument where the new line passes nearest **[live 8.3.10]**, **[build]**.

A split at an inner point is one batch too: the line keeps the part from its start to the split point, the new line ("L-SWD 2") the part from there to the end, and each instrument goes to the part it sits on (the nearer; the first part on a tie) at its distance from that part's start (`lineSplitBatch`, `splitAtBatch`). On the dev stack, L-SWD with a point added between PT-1 and PT-2 at about 1.8 km and split there keeps PT-1 at 1.1 km and puts LV-1 at about 0.2 km and PT-2 at about 1.1 km on L-SWD 2 **[live 8.3.10]** (`e2e/tests/geometry.spec.mjs`); the unit tests split the test vectors' L-SWD at its bend the same way **[build]**. The nodes and segments of each part follow from its own `ends` and attachments as above: the split point is a floating end of both. One Undo puts the line back, removes the new part and returns every instrument to the line, in one reverse batch **[live 8.3.10]**.

## Adding and moving an instrument in the studio (epic 4 entry 9)

An instrument is made and moved from its line, never placed loose (owner, 2026-11-06, entry 3; WCAG 2.5.7): there is no instrument tool on the toolbar, and instrument kinds are not among the Point tool's kinds or the kind buttons (`web/typescript/studio.ts` `kindsFor`, `kindButtons`).

- **A line's Details** (a line, not an area), after Location: **Instruments**, its instruments by name in the line popup's order (`instrumentsOn`: distance, then id) with the popup's distance ("PT-1 1.1 km", "LV-1 2 km", "PT-2 2.8 km"), a click on one selecting it with its Details; "None yet" when there is none. **Add an instrument**: with one instrument kind in the kind list it arms that kind at once; with several a short list of the instrument kinds opens to pick from (Esc closes it). Off, saying why, while the store is unreachable, for a line not saved yet, and with no instrument kind in the kind list.
- **Placing.** While armed the drawing bar reads "New tap on L-SWD - click the line or type its distance", with a distance field in metres and the line's length beside it ("of 2,922 m"), Place (Enter in the field) and Cancel (Esc); the line's handles step aside and the map takes a crosshair. A click within 12 px of the line (`INSTRUMENT_CLICK_PX`) places the instrument at the click's projection (`geometry.ts` `project`); a click anywhere else, the empty map, another line or a marker, is refused in the bar, "Click on L-SWD: an instrument sits on its line", and nothing is saved. A typed distance from 0 to the line's length places it there; anything else is refused, "Type a distance from 0 to 2,922 m". Picking a tool, another selection or Edit mode left puts it down too.
- **The save.** One `edits.apply` batch with one create: the kind, the kind's name as the placeholder, the kind's prefill, the point at the distance (6 decimals) and `attachment: {lineId, distanceM}` (0.1 m), as `placeOp` writes them (`instrumentCreateBatch`); one undo step "add Tap". It is built from the line's stored record when it goes, so it waits while a save of the line is in flight; a change of the line's shape or its delete waits in turn for a save that attaches an instrument to it, so the line's batch carries it. Then the new instrument takes the selection and its Details open with Name focused, as a placed pad's do.
- **An instrument's Details.** Location reads "On L-SWD at 1.1 km", then **Distance along the line (m)** with the line's length beside it: Enter or blur moves it there (one update, "move PT-1"); a distance outside the line is refused beside the field and nothing is saved. Latitude and longitude are read only and there is no Move; the Kind select lists instrument kinds only (a point's lists the other point kinds).
- **Dragging.** A selected instrument drags with the Select tool in Edit mode (own handling, not Terra Draw's handles): a press within 12 px of its marker starts it and the map does not pan; the pointer is projected onto its line throughout, so the place shown (a disc in the selection token) never leaves the line, and a pointer off the line projects back; snapping never applies. The release writes one update with point and attachment, "move PT-1"; Esc during the drag lets it go with nothing sent. The map's `data-instrument-handle` names the instrument that drags and `data-instrument-drag` the metres along the line while it does.
- **Everything else stays.** Reshaping, moving, splitting and deleting a line carry its instruments as before (entries 5 and 8, above), the new ones with them; the Gateway's attachment rules ([contract.md](contract.md), "Instruments on lines") judge every save, and a refusal shows on the save status row. On the dev stack (`e2e/tests/addinstrument.spec.mjs`, 1366 px): a tap clicked onto L-SWD near PT-1, a meter typed at 2,841 m (listed in the row and the line popup), PT-1 dragged along and off the line and typed back to 1,143 m, each one step, and an added tap kept on L-SWD reshaped, deleted with it and restored by one Undo. The dev kind list carries a third instrument kind, Meter (`meter`, drawn with the core set's new `meter` glyph, a flow meter on a line), with no kind setup.
